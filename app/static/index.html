ALERTS, STRAIGHT TO YOUR PHONE

Connect Telegram

  1. Create a bot with @BotFather using /newbot.
  2. Open your bot in Telegram and send /start.
  3. Find your chat ID using Telegram’s getUpdates API. Follow the instructions in docs/RUNNING.md.
  4. In Job Watcher, open Telegram delivery and save the bot token and chat ID in your personal settings.
  5. Select Send test message to verify your connection.

Your bot token is stored on the server for your account and is never shown again after saving.
